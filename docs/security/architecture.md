# Security design
Parents: Firebase sign-in followed by server token verification; no protected endpoints are present yet. Family policies deny access unless active membership and role permit the operation. Never trust a family_id or device_id supplied by the browser. Device pairing needs authenticated parent initiation, short expiry, rate limits, device-side disclosure, one-time atomic redemption and scoped rotating device credentials. Keep Android credentials in Keystore-backed encryption; no secrets in SharedPreferences or source.

Consent is purpose-specific and versioned; server grants cannot override Android OS permission. Revocation and unpairing stop synchronization and sessions immediately and invalidate device credentials. Screen sharing requires per-session consent and session expiry. Location uploads require both explicit sharing consent and current platform permission.

Use TLS in production, encrypted managed disks/backups, secret manager credentials and minimal privileges. Keep private data out of logs and FCM payloads. Audit pairing, access changes, consent changes and session start/stop with actor and time; append-only access controls and tamper resistance are later deployment work. Proposed retention pending product approval: location 7 days, aggregated usage 30 days, security audit 90 days; scheduled deletion must include replicas/backups under documented policy. Provide family export/deletion and legal guardian/age requirements before release. No retention job exists in Phase 1.
